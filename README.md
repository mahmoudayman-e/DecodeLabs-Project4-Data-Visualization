# Project 4: Data Visualization with Python

## Project Overview

This is the fourth project completed as part of the **DecodeLabs Data Analytics Internship**.

The project focuses on transforming sales data into clear and actionable visual insights using Python. The goal is not only to visualize data, but also to identify trends, communicate key findings, and provide business recommendations that support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- GitHub

---

## Dataset

The project uses the cleaned sales dataset generated during the previous **DecodeLabs Data Analytics projects**.

The dataset contains **1,200 sales records** and includes information about:

- Order ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Product
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Payment Method
- Order Status
- Coupon Code
- Referral Source
- Total Price
- Other order-related information

---

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend

A line chart was used to analyze revenue fluctuations over time and identify high- and low-performing periods.

**Insight:**  
Monthly revenue shows noticeable fluctuations across different periods.

**So What?**  
These fluctuations may indicate seasonal or time-based changes in sales performance.

**Recommended Action:**  
Investigate the drivers behind high- and low-performing months to improve sales forecasting and planning.

---

### 2. Top 10 Products by Revenue

A horizontal bar chart was used to identify the products generating the highest revenue.

**Insight:**  
A small group of products contributes significantly to overall revenue.

**So What?**  
These products are important contributors to business performance.

**Recommended Action:**  
Prioritize high-revenue products in inventory planning and promotional campaigns.

---

### 3. Revenue by Payment Method

A bar chart was used to compare revenue generated through different payment methods.

**Insight:**  
Digital payment methods, particularly Online and Credit Card payments, generate strong revenue.

**So What?**  
Customers show a strong preference for digital payment options.

**Recommended Action:**  
Continue optimizing digital payment methods and improve the checkout experience.

---

### 4. Order Status Distribution

A bar chart was used to analyze the distribution of different order statuses.

**Insight:**  
Cancelled orders represent a significant portion of the dataset.

**So What?**  
A high cancellation level may indicate potential operational or customer experience issues.

**Recommended Action:**  
Investigate the main reasons for cancellations and develop strategies to improve order completion.

---

### 5. Total Revenue Distribution by Quantity

A boxplot was used to examine the distribution of order revenue across different order quantities.

**Insight:**  
Higher quantities generally correspond to higher order values, while also showing greater revenue variation.

**So What?**  
Larger purchases can contribute significantly to overall revenue.

**Recommended Action:**  
Encourage larger purchases through bundle offers, volume discounts, and cross-selling strategies.

---

### 6. Orders by Referral Source

A bar chart was used to compare the number of orders generated by different referral sources.

**Insight:**  
Instagram is the most common referral source in the dataset.

**So What?**  
Instagram appears to be an important customer acquisition channel.

**Recommended Action:**  
Strengthen marketing activities on high-performing referral channels while monitoring their conversion and profitability.

---

## Executive Summary

The visualizations provide an overview of sales performance across revenue, products, payment methods, order statuses, purchasing quantities, and referral sources.

The analysis highlights important variations in monthly revenue, differences in product performance, customer payment preferences, order status patterns, and referral channel performance.

These findings provide opportunities to improve revenue performance, reduce cancelled orders, optimize payment methods, and strengthen customer acquisition strategies.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. Investigate the reasons behind cancelled and returned orders.
2. Prioritize high-revenue products in inventory and promotional planning.
3. Continue optimizing digital payment channels.
4. Analyze monthly revenue patterns to improve forecasting.
5. Strengthen marketing efforts through high-performing referral sources.
6. Encourage larger purchases using bundles and volume-based offers.
7. Monitor lower-performing products and marketing channels for improvement.

This project demonstrates how Python-based data visualization can transform sales data into clear and actionable business insights.

By combining appropriate visualizations with business-focused interpretation, the project goes beyond presenting numbers and helps identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement.

The project demonstrates the importance of effective data visualization in communicating insights and supporting data-driven business decisions.
